1. What Bloopers VR is

Bloopers VR is a VR obstacle-racing party game. Solo Practice works fully offline. Multiplayer (Quick Match, Host, Join by code) connects you to other players in a shared room for 3-round matches.<[...]

2. Data we collect

2.1 Played on-device (stored on your headset only)

  • Display name. Defaults to Blooper. If you edit it, we save it locally under your Meta user ID (bloopers.name.{userId}). 3–12 characters. Not required t[...]
  • Blooper color / appearance choice. Saved locally (bloopers.color.{userId}).
  • Best times, coins, game progress. Solo best times and local coin/reward state are stored in on-device app storage (PlayerPrefs / local app data). In v1 coins are loca[...].
  • Fallback ID and settings. If Meta login is unavailable (e.g. in the Unity Editor or before setup), a random local ID and mute/block preferences are stored on-device.

Uninstalling the app deletes this local data. Reinstalling or switching headsets resets your name to Blooper.

2.2 Meta Platform data (Quest only)

  • App entitlement check. At startup we call Meta Platform SDK Entitlements.IsUserEntitledToApplication to verify you own the app or trial.
  • Meta per-app user ID. We call Users.GetLoggedInUser().ID to tell players apart in a room and to scope your local name/color. This uses only the User ID and is not a profile account tie-out beyond your headset.
  • Store purchases / trial status. Payments are processed by Meta through the Horizon Store. We never see or store your card number or billing details.

Meta processes this data under the Meta Privacy Policy and Meta Platform terms.

2.3 Multiplayer data (only when you join online play)

Multiplayer uses NormalVR Normcore rooms, including Quick Match and private rooms joined by code. When you play online, the following is transmitted in real time to Normcore and to[...]

  • Display name (sanitized on receipt), color ID, spawn slot
  • Body position and body yaw, movement state (still / walk / run / grounded), jump events
  • Checkpoint index, finish / elimination state, finish time
  • Room code / room name, match round and timing, ghost-bot IDs for filling empty slots
  • Network connection data needed to run the room (such as IP address), handled by Normcore as a service provider

Solo Practice never connects to Normcore and transmits nothing.

2.4 Voice chat (multiplayer only, real-time only)

  • Microphone audio. If you grant microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO), your live voice is streamed to the nearest players in your room (limited to the ~3 nearest speakers in proximity voice mode).
  • We do not record, transcribe, or store voice on our servers. Other players hear it live and then it is gone.
  • You can deny the mic permission and still play, without voice. In-game wrist menu provides self-mute, per-player mute/block, and report.

3. Data we do NOT collect

  • No third-party analytics (no Firebase Analytics events in v1).
  • No crash-report user profiling in v1. The project may contain unused Firebase libraries, but v1 does not initialize Firebase Auth, Firestore, Functions, or transmit gameplay data to Firebase.
  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no cookies, no cross-app tracking.
  • No precise geolocation, no contacts, no photo library access.
  • No hand-tracking or eye-tracking data. Controllers only.
  • No payment details collected by us — handled by Meta.

4. How we use data

  • Run the game: spawn you, move your Blooper, score rounds, grant local coins.
  • Run multiplayer: matchmake rooms, sync players and hazards, relay live voice.
  • Safety: enforce mute / block / report, sanitize display names.
  • Verify ownership and trial status via Meta.

We do not use your data for advertising or sell or share it with data brokers.

5. Third-party services

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — Horizon OS, Horizon Store, Meta XR SDK and Meta Platform SDK (entitlement, user ID, purchases). Meta Privacy Policy
  • Normal Inc. (Normcore). — Real-time multiplayer rooms, avatar sync, voice relay. Normcore Privacy Policy

No other third-party SDK receives personal data in v1.

6. Data retention

  • On-device name, color, coins, best times: kept until you clear app data, uninstall, or reset them in-game.
  • Multiplayer room and voice data: kept only for the life of the room to run the match, then discarded by us. Normcore and Meta may retain connection logs per their own policies.
  • Blocked-player lists and mute settings: stored on-device until cleared.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Play Solo Practice without creating any online connection.
  • Change or reset your display name at any time (empty = Blooper).
  • Deny or revoke microphone permission in Quest Settings to play without voice.
  • Use in-game mute / block / report during multiplayer.
  • Delete local data by uninstalling the app.
  • Contact us at rangeshus@gmail.com to request help with a privacy question. We will respond within a reasonable time. Meta-account or store-billing questions should also go to Meta Support.

8. Children

Bloopers VR is a bright, non-violent party game intended for a general teen-through-adult casual audience for short sessions. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Security

We use Meta entitlement checks, Normcore room isolation by versioned room names/codes, input sanitization for display names, and client-minimal networking (body position/yaw and match state only).

10. International transfers

Multiplayer rooms may be hosted or relayed outside your country by Normcore to connect players. By playing online you understand your match data and live voice will be routed as needed to run the room.

11. Changes to this policy

If v1 changes — for example if we enable cloud saves, Firebase, analytics, crash reporting, or in-app purchases beyond the Meta store trial/paid flow — we will update this page and change the effective date.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or a deletion request: rangeshus@gmail.com.
