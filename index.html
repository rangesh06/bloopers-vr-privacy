1. About this policy

Rangesh U S, operating as Spawn Bridge, operates Bloopers VR, a VR obstacle-racing party game with Solo Practice and optional online multiplayer, including Quick Match, hosting a room, and joining by room code. Rangesh U S is the data controller responsible for determining the purposes and means of processing the personal data described in this policy.

In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Rangesh U S, operating as Spawn Bridge.

This policy explains what information Bloopers VR processes, why it is used, where it is stored or transmitted, and how you can request deletion. Processing includes information used temporarily during gameplay, information saved on your headset, and information transmitted to operate multiplayer.

2. Information we process

2.1 Meta Platform information

  • Meta app-scoped User ID: Bloopers VR requests the current user's unique app-scoped account identifier through the Meta Platform SDK. We use this identifier to recognize the account across sessions and associate saved preferences and rewards with the correct account. It is cached locally and included in multiplayer player synchronization. Although it is not your real name, it is an account identifier and is treated as personal data.
  • App entitlement information: The app checks with Meta whether your account is entitled to use Bloopers VR. This check is used to verify access to the app.
  • Meta username and profile photo: The current release does not use your Meta username or profile photo to display your in-game identity. Your in-game display name is chosen within Bloopers VR.
  • Store purchases: Payments for the app through the Meta Horizon Store are processed by Meta. We do not receive your payment-card number or banking details.

2.2 Information stored on your headset

  • Chosen in-game display name: If you enter a name, we save it locally and associate it with your account identifier. A default name is used if you do not choose one.
  • Character color and appearance preferences: We save your selected Blooper color locally and associate it with your account.
  • Gameplay progress and rewards: Local app storage holds saved gameplay information, such as best times and coin/reward data. These local saves are not a developer-operated cloud-save service.
  • Cached identifiers and settings: The app saves information needed to restore your player identity and preferences. In supported development or fallback environments, it may use a locally generated identifier instead of a Meta User ID.

2.3 Online multiplayer information

Multiplayer uses Normcore, provided by Normal Inc. When you join online play, information is transmitted through Normcore and synchronized with other players' game clients as needed to operate the session. This includes:

  • Your account identifier, chosen in-game display name, character color, and player/spawn assignment.
  • Character position, orientation, movement state, and jump events.
  • Match progress, checkpoints, finish or elimination state, and finish times.
  • Room identifiers or codes, round state, timing, and game-generated bot identifiers.
  • Technical connection information, such as your IP address, processed by the multiplayer service to establish and operate network connections.

Other players can see your chosen name, character appearance, movement, and relevant match results. Your account identifier is included in synchronized player data even though it is not intended to be displayed as your player name.

Solo Practice does not require joining a Normcore multiplayer room. Meta Platform ownership and identity checks are separate from multiplayer connections.

2.4 Privacy and support correspondence

If you contact us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any information you provide to help us answer your question or identify and verify a deletion request.

2.5 Voice chat

Voice chat is not included in this release. Bloopers VR does not collect, transmit, record, or transcribe microphone audio for voice chat.

3. Information we do not use

  • We do not use player data for advertising, targeted advertising, or cross-app tracking.
  • We do not sell personal data or provide it to data brokers.
  • This release does not use Firebase Analytics events or initialize Firebase Auth, Firestore, or Functions to transmit gameplay data.
  • We do not request access to your contacts, photo library, or precise geographic location.
  • This release uses controllers rather than hand-tracking or eye-tracking data.
  • We do not receive payment-card or banking details from Meta Store purchases.

4. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Verify your entitlement to access Bloopers VR.
  • Identify your account consistently across gameplay sessions.
  • Save and restore account-associated names, character preferences, progress, and rewards.
  • Connect multiplayer sessions and synchronize players, character movement, and match state.
  • Calculate match results and award local gameplay rewards.
  • Validate and sanitize player-selected names and other game inputs.
  • Respond to support questions and process privacy and deletion requests.

5. Service providers and disclosure

  • Meta: Provides the Meta Quest platform, Horizon Store, account identity, and app entitlement services. Meta independently processes information under its own policies. Meta Privacy Policy .
  • Normal Inc. / Normcore: Provides multiplayer networking and player-state synchronization. Information necessary to operate online sessions is transmitted through this service. Normcore Privacy Policy .
  • Email service providers: Process messages sent to our privacy and support address so that we can receive and respond to requests.

We may also disclose information when necessary to comply with applicable law or a valid legal request. We do not sell personal data.

6. Data retention

  • Local app data: Saved identifiers, names, preferences, progress, and rewards remain in your headset's app storage until removed through an available reset or app-data deletion method.
  • Multiplayer state: Player and match state is processed to operate multiplayer sessions. Service-provider retention of connection information, logs, or other records is governed by the applicable provider's practices and our service arrangements.
  • Support and privacy requests: We retain correspondence as needed to resolve the request and document its handling. Information may be retained longer where necessary to meet a legal obligation.

Headset or platform backup features may preserve or restore local app data. Uninstalling and reinstalling the app therefore should not be assumed to permanently delete every copy of saved data. Contact us for help identifying the appropriate deletion steps.

7. Your rights and how to request deletion

All users, regardless of country or geographic location, may request deletion of their personal data free of charge.

Email: hello@spawnbridge.com
Suggested subject: Bloopers Data Deletion Request

How to submit a request

  1. Email us and state that you want your Bloopers VR personal data deleted.
  2. Include your in-game display name and describe any data you specifically want removed, if known. You do not need to know your numeric Meta User ID to start a request.
  3. If additional information is reasonably necessary to identify the account or verify ownership, we will explain what is needed. Do not send your Meta password or payment-card details.

How we handle your request

  • We will identify and delete personal data associated with your account that is held by us.
  • Where applicable, we will coordinate deletion of data held on our behalf by service providers.
  • For information stored only on your headset, we will provide instructions for removing the local app data. We cannot remotely erase files from your headset.
  • We will confirm when the request has been completed. If any information must be retained to meet a legal obligation, we will explain the information retained and the reason.

What deletion means

Deleting local Bloopers VR data may remove your chosen display name, character preferences, saved times, and coin/reward progress. Deletion of Bloopers VR data does not delete your Meta account, store ownership, or information independently maintained by Meta.

If you use the app again after deletion, it may retrieve your Meta app-scoped User ID again and create new local or multiplayer data.

Other choices

  • You may choose Solo Practice instead of joining online multiplayer.
  • You may change or reset your chosen in-game display name.
  • You may contact us to ask about your personal data, request correction, or exercise other rights available under applicable law.

8. Children

Bloopers VR is intended for a teen-through-adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data to us, contact hello@spawnbridge.com so that we can investigate and address the information.

9. Security

We use measures such as app entitlement checks, multiplayer room separation, and validation of player-selected display names to support the operation and security of the app. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. International processing

We operate from India. Our service providers may process multiplayer connections and support correspondence in countries other than your own. These countries may have different data-protection laws. Your ability to request deletion from us is available regardless of your location.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our data practices change, including if we introduce cloud saves, analytics, voice chat, additional Meta profile usage, or in-app purchases. The effective date above identifies the latest revision. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
